Who We Are

VIA is an audiovisual production agency built by filmmakers, designers, programmers, prompt engineers, screenwriters, musicians, and sound producers who thrive on experimentation. We make professional videos using AI tools to generate image, video, music, voices, and more—always guided by human taste and craftsmanship.

Our workflow blends field production with advanced generative systems, so every project benefits from bespoke direction, art, and sound while tapping into the speed and scale of AI. We are the people you call when you want cinematic storytelling with the agility of cutting-edge technology.

We have created for music bands, giant streaming platforms, pioneering science communicators, and even governmental cultural programs. Whether it is scripting, character design, live-action shoots, animation, or editing, our teams own the full pipeline and deliver ready-to-air content that feels alive.

Our Productions

Lucha Clandestina

Pilot episode for renowned science network Xplora Snacks de Ciencia. Lucha roams a post-apocalyptic Quequén searching for forbidden knowledge. VIA handled scripting, character design, animation, and sound design.

Client: Gelatina

A dreamy pilot for streaming giant Gelatina inspired by Borges and Cortázar. VIA crafted the visual language, character design, animation, and immersive sound to match the channel's massive fanbase.

Commissioned by Anda Calabaza with Fundación Sí, this animated version of Argentina's anthem uses a narrative we scripted to invite children into the story, with original character design, animation, and editing by VIA.
